# ComPAS source status

Catalog entry 140 remains pending. Its primary citation is Tang R, Shen J,
and Yuan Y, *ComPAS: A Bayesian drug combination platform trial design with
adaptive shrinkage*, Statistics in Medicine 38(7):1120–1134 (2019),
[DOI 10.1002/sim.8026](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8026),
[PubMed 30419609](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30419609/).

The abstract describes adaptive borrowing across combinations using Bayesian
model selection and hierarchical models. The platform can drop futile
combinations, graduate effective combinations and add new ones. This does not
establish the outcome likelihood, exchangeability assumptions, model-space
prior, hyperpriors, posterior computation, allocation or stopping thresholds.

A bounded read-only review on September 28, 2026 could not retrieve the
[official app](https://biostatistics.mdanderson.org/shinyapps/ComPAS) or the
publisher's full article. The PAS navigation card at
[trialdesign.org](https://trialdesign.org/) exposed only a shell through the
available reader; no author source archive or open full manuscript was found.
No app model was executed and no native numeric output was obtained.

A faithful port needs the primary method and operating rules, prior/calibration
inputs, and numerical examples or native outputs. Existing BCHM and platform
allocation modules offer possible computational building blocks, but their
statistical assumptions do not establish ComPAS behavior. An independent-arm
beta-binomial monitor would not resolve this source gap.

On September 29 the authors' public home page was retrieved successfully. Its
ComPAS card still linked only to `one-page-shell.html#ComPAS`, and the publication
list provided the citation without a manuscript or source download. The Wiley
article remained unavailable to the reader. No institutional app was requested:
a saved browser permission currently blocks the MD Anderson domain. The primary
method and its numerical constants therefore remain unrecovered.
