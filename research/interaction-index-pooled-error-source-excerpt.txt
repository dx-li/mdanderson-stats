The paper gives the delta relation

    Var(logit(y)) ~= Var(y) / [y * (1-y)]^2.

For a combination effect without replicate variance, it says to substitute the
average squared residuals from all involved single-agent median-effect fits for
this transformed-scale variance, assuming common variance for single-agent and
combination effects. The log-index interval uses t degrees of freedom n-2k,
where n is the total single-agent observation count and there are 2k fitted
single-agent coefficients.
