## UAROET current-guide contracts requiring integration

The current guide includes efficacy futility, not just the paper API's toxicity
screen. Resolve both posterior cutoffs and final/interim behavior from native
controller instructions before adding a current-version profile. Calibration
uses100 pseudo-patients per dose and averages posterior parameter means over
pseudo-trials. ESS uses beta moment matching of marginal ordinal probabilities
across active doses/categories, reporting mean and maximum. Do not substitute
joint-cell ESS. Association is Uniform(-1,1).

Open cohorts use trailing identical dose assignments, including pending patients;
check safety every patient. Only toxicity-known partial records contribute to the
likelihood; efficacy-only outcomes wait for toxicity. Delayed outcomes are
independent uniforms within configured windows; arrivals are exponential.
Hybrid greedy/randomized allocation, alternative randomization weights, surplus,
minimum probability best and shrinking delta all need native controller evidence.
Adaptive sampling targets MCSE ratios; a fixed short chain is not equivalent.
Original patient files use one-based patient/dose and zero-based outcomes, with-1
pending. Native prior files use threshold-major dose coordinates, unlike the
existing Python order. Three-dose/four-outcome files have19 values; map explicitly.

The guide says UseHybridGreedyArandAssignment while an example says
UseHybridGreedyArandAlgorithm. Resolve parser behavior rather than silently
accepting both as native behavior. Hidden lognormal-increment and correlation
options require scope/default review. Native thresholds occupy five coordinates
regardless of active dose count; efficacy at0/5/10, toxicity15/20/25, association30.

Screening accepts equality at posterior cutoffs. Above-cutoff toxicity excludes a
dose only when that dose has observed toxicity or a lower dose was already
excluded for toxicity. Propagation still requires the higher dose's posterior
tail to exceed its cutoff. Futility also has an observed-failure gate. Utility
filters have a minimum-observation gate, and a separate minimum-total-toxicity
fallback can reinstate the lowest dose after all doses were excluded. These are
compatibility contracts, not safe default recommendations. Selector initialization
5e0d0/5e0dd/5e0ed sets the three gate fields to3; application setters may override
these defaults and their complete transfer still needs review.

Hybrid selection chooses the highest-index exact mean-utility tie. It remains
greedy unless the best dose also has the highest allocation and its surplus over
the second-highest allocation reaches the requested threshold. Weighted sampling
uses mean utility or supplied good-outcome probabilities; equality at cumulative
probability boundaries selects the earlier acceptable dose. Zero total weight is
not covered by these fixtures and must not silently produce an arbitrary dose.

old cohort checks its own size. With first/new/old sizes3/2/3 this can extend the
starting-dose run to four assignments. Guide-correct safe behavior must remain
separate. Saved-history reconstruction at5e150 and subsequent conduct may differ
from the live stateful simulation and need their own traces.
